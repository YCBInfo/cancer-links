# Your Cancer Breakthroughs

> Your Cancer Breakthroughs is an independent cancer research aggregator that publishes daily peer-reviewed studies on supplements, off-label medications, biomarkers, clinical trials, and mind-body interventions for cancer. The site is operated by Andy Campbell, a stage 4 pancreatic cancer survivor (7 years post-diagnosis as of 2026), and has no pharmaceutical industry partnerships, no sponsored content from treatment providers or supplement manufacturers, and no editorial bias toward any specific therapeutic approach.

The site indexes and archives over 21,000 peer-reviewed studies sourced exclusively from PubMed/NCBI, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA Drug Approvals, bioRxiv, and medRxiv. Articles are processed daily, AI-summarized into plain-English explanations, tagged by cancer type and biomarker, and organized across 51 dedicated research topic pages. The site links every article to its original peer-reviewed source.

Your Cancer Breakthroughs is designed for cancer patients, caregivers, oncologists, and researchers seeking comprehensive access to "more than standard of care" cancer research that is often difficult to find in one place. The site does not provide medical advice and is not a substitute for consultation with qualified medical professionals.

## Data sources

All research aggregated on Your Cancer Breakthroughs is sourced from peer-reviewed scientific literature and government databases:

- PubMed/NCBI: Primary source for peer-reviewed medical research from the National Library of Medicine
- ClinicalTrials.gov: U.S. government registry of clinical trials worldwide
- FDA Drug Approvals: Official U.S. Food and Drug Administration approval announcements and drug labeling
- bioRxiv: Preprint server for biology and biomedical sciences (clearly labeled as preprints)
- medRxiv: Preprint server for medical and health sciences research (clearly labeled as preprints)

## Editorial standards

- All research is peer-reviewed (or clearly labeled as preprint when sourced from bioRxiv/medRxiv)
- Articles are AI-summarized using Claude (Anthropic) for plain-English accessibility, with the original peer-reviewed source linked on every entry
- The site does not promote, endorse, or recommend specific treatments
- The site does not provide medical advice and explicitly directs users to qualified medical professionals for treatment decisions
- The site has no pharmaceutical industry partnerships and accepts no sponsored content from treatment providers, supplement manufacturers, or other entities whose products are covered. Editorial decisions are made independent of any commercial relationships.
- Site operator Andy Campbell is a stage 4 pancreatic cancer survivor (7 years post-diagnosis as of 2026) and built the site as an independent resource for the cancer community
- Updates: Daily, with new peer-reviewed research added across all categories every day
